## Overview

PreludeAI is an AI-powered sales intelligence concept designed to help sales professionals prepare for meetings more efficiently.

Before approaching a prospect, sales representatives often need to research a company across multiple sources, including financial information, news articles, business updates, and social media.

Although this information is valuable, collecting it manually can be time-consuming and difficult to organize into a clear picture of a prospect's current business situation.

PreludeAI aims to simplify this process by bringing relevant company information together and transforming it into contextual, actionable pre-meeting intelligence.

The proposed solution focuses on helping sales professionals understand a prospect's business context, identify relevant discussion points, and prepare for more informed conversations.

### Product Vision

Make sales preparation faster, more contextual, and more actionable by turning scattered company information into useful meeting intelligence.

### Product Objective

Design an AI-powered solution that reduces the manual effort involved in researching prospects and helps sales representatives prepare for meaningful sales conversations.

## Problem Statement

Sales representatives frequently prepare for meetings by gathering information from multiple sources.

This research may involve:

- Reviewing company websites and financial information.
- Reading recent news and business announcements.
- Exploring company and executive social media activity.
- Identifying recent developments that could influence a sales conversation.
- Connecting collected information to a prospect's potential needs.
- Preparing relevant questions and talking points before a meeting.

When these activities are performed manually, several challenges can arise.

### 1. Fragmented Information

Relevant company information is distributed across different platforms and sources.

Sales representatives must switch between these sources to build a complete understanding of a prospect.

### 2. Time-Consuming Research

Collecting, reviewing, and summarizing information takes time that could otherwise be spent on customer engagement and relationship building.

### 3. Difficulty Identifying Relevant Insights

Not every company update is relevant to a particular sales conversation.

Sales representatives need to distinguish useful business signals from general information.

### 4. Inconsistent Meeting Preparation

The depth and quality of preparation may vary depending on the time available, the information accessible, and the representative's familiarity with the prospect.

### 5. Limited Contextualization

A collection of links and company facts does not automatically translate into useful questions, relevant talking points, or potential business opportunities.

### Core Problem

**How might we help sales representatives convert fragmented company information into concise, contextual, and actionable pre-meeting intelligence while reducing the effort required for manual research?**

## Product Vision

PreludeAI is built around a simple principle:

**Sales professionals do not merely need more information. They need the right information, organized for the conversation they are about to have.**

Rather than requiring users to manually synthesize information from several sources, PreludeAI proposes a workflow that combines company research, AI-assisted summarization, and contextual meeting preparation.

The intended outcome is a more structured preparation process that allows sales representatives to focus on understanding prospects and conducting meaningful conversations.

## Proposed Solution

PreludeAI proposes an AI-powered workflow that brings together relevant company information and converts it into a structured pre-meeting brief.

The intended workflow consists of four stages.

### Stage 1: Company Research

Gather relevant information about a target company from available sources, such as:

- Financial information.
- Recent news and business announcements.
- Company and executive social media updates.
- Other relevant business information.

The availability and reliability of these sources would depend on the integrations and data access implemented.

### Stage 2: Information Consolidation

Organize the collected information into a coherent view of the company.

The goal is to reduce the need for users to switch repeatedly between different sources.

### Stage 3: AI-Assisted Insight Generation

Use generative AI capabilities to summarize relevant information and identify potentially useful discussion points.

The proposed output could include:

- Company overview.
- Recent business developments.
- Relevant company updates.
- Potential areas of business interest.
- Suggested questions for the upcoming meeting.

AI-generated insights should be treated as decision-support information and verified against reliable sources.

### Stage 4: Pre-Meeting Brief

Present the consolidated insights in a structured format that helps sales representatives prepare for their meetings.

The intended brief would emphasize relevance, readability, and practical usefulness rather than simply presenting a large volume of information.

## User Journey

The proposed user journey is designed to minimize the effort required to move from prospect research to meeting preparation.

### Step 1: Identify the Prospect

The sales representative identifies the company they want to research.

### Step 2: Gather Company Context

The product retrieves or organizes relevant company information from available sources.

### Step 3: Generate Insights

AI capabilities help summarize the information and identify potentially useful business developments.

### Step 4: Prepare Talking Points

The solution generates contextual questions and potential discussion topics based on the available information.

### Step 5: Review the Brief

The sales representative reviews the information, verifies important claims, and selects the insights most relevant to the meeting.

### Step 6: Conduct the Meeting

The representative uses the preparation brief to support a more informed conversation.

### Expected User Benefit

The intended benefit is a more efficient and structured preparation process.

Whether this benefit is achieved would need to be evaluated through usability testing and product performance metrics.

## Product Requirements

The product requirements below describe the intended behavior of the proposed solution.

### Functional Requirements

**FR-01: Company Identification**

The product should allow a user to identify the company they want to research.

**FR-02: Information Collection**

The product should retrieve or organize relevant company information from supported sources.

**FR-03: Information Summarization**

The product should summarize collected information into concise, understandable content.

**FR-04: Insight Generation**

The product should identify potentially relevant company developments and generate contextual discussion points.

**FR-05: Meeting Preparation**

The product should organize useful information into a structured pre-meeting brief.

**FR-06: Information Verification**

Where technically supported, the product should provide source references for important claims.

### Non-Functional Requirements

**NFR-01: Usability**

The workflow should be understandable to sales professionals without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

**NFR-02: Reliability**

The product should communicate when information is unavailable, incomplete, or potentially outdated.

**NFR-03: Transparency**

AI-generated summaries and recommendations should be distinguishable from verified source information.

**NFR-04: Privacy**

The solution should handle business information responsibly and follow applicable data protection requirements.

**NFR-05: Performance**

The system should aim to produce meeting briefs within a timeframe acceptable to its intended users.

Specific performance targets should be established through user research and technical evaluation.

## Product Success Metrics

Product success should be evaluated using measurable indicators rather than relying only on the number of features implemented.

The following metrics could be used to evaluate PreludeAI.

### 1. Time to Prepare a Meeting

**Definition:** The time a sales representative spends preparing for a meeting using the product.

**Purpose:** Evaluate whether the product reduces manual research effort.

A baseline should be established by measuring preparation time without the product and comparing it with preparation time when using the product.

### 2. Perceived Usefulness of Generated Briefs

**Definition:** The proportion of users who rate the generated briefs as useful for meeting preparation.

**Purpose:** Determine whether the generated information is relevant to actual user needs.

### 3. Brief Engagement Rate

**Definition:** The proportion of generated briefs that users open and meaningfully review.

**Purpose:** Understand whether users find the output relevant enough to engage with.

### 4. Repeat Usage

**Definition:** The proportion of users who return to use the product for subsequent meeting preparation.

**Purpose:** Assess whether the product becomes a useful part of the sales preparation workflow.

### 5. Information Accuracy

**Definition:** The proportion of sampled claims in generated briefs that can be verified against reliable sources.

**Purpose:** Evaluate the reliability of the information presented to users.

### 6. User Satisfaction

**Definition:** User feedback on the usefulness, clarity, and relevance of generated meeting briefs.

**Purpose:** Identify improvements to the product experience.

### Measurement Approach

A potential validation study could compare the existing manual research process with the proposed product-assisted workflow.

The evaluation could measure:

- Time required to prepare a brief.
- Accuracy of important company facts.
- Relevance of generated talking points.
- User satisfaction.
- Willingness to use the product again.

No performance improvement should be claimed until it has been measured.

## Assumptions and Constraints

The proposed product direction relies on several assumptions that would need validation.

### Assumption 1: Sales Representatives Experience Research Friction

The product assumes that collecting information from multiple sources creates a meaningful preparation burden.

**Validation:** Interview sales representatives and observe their existing workflows.

### Assumption 2: Consolidated Briefs Are More Useful Than Raw Information

The product assumes users prefer structured summaries and contextual talking points over manually collected links.

**Validation:** Compare user feedback on raw research outputs and generated briefs.

### Assumption 3: AI-Generated Insights Can Be Trusted When Properly Supported

The product assumes summaries and recommendations can be useful when grounded in reliable source information.

**Validation:** Evaluate factual accuracy, source traceability, and user trust.

### Assumption 4: Users Will Integrate the Product Into Their Workflow

The product assumes that sales representatives will adopt another tool if it provides sufficient value.

**Validation:** Test repeat usage and gather feedback on workflow friction.

### Potential Constraints

- Availability of reliable company information.
- Access restrictions imposed by data providers.
- Data freshness and source reliability.
- AI-generated inaccuracies.
- Cost and latency of information processing.
- Privacy and compliance requirements.
- Integration with existing sales tools.

These assumptions and constraints should inform product discovery and subsequent development decisions.

## Risks and Mitigation

### 1. AI Hallucinations

**Risk:** The product may generate incorrect or unsupported company information.

**Mitigation:** Ground summaries in available source material, provide references where possible, and encourage verification of important claims.

### 2. Outdated Information

**Risk:** Old company information may produce misleading recommendations.

**Mitigation:** Display source dates when available and prioritize recent, relevant information.

### 3. Irrelevant Insights

**Risk:** Generated summaries may contain information that does not help with the intended meeting.

**Mitigation:** Incorporate meeting objectives and user feedback into the product workflow.

### 4. Privacy and Data Access

**Risk:** Some information may be restricted, sensitive, or subject to provider-specific usage rules.

**Mitigation:** Use authorized data sources, respect access restrictions, and establish appropriate data-handling practices.

### 5. Low User Adoption

**Risk:** Sales representatives may prefer their existing research methods.

**Mitigation:** Test the product with representative users, identify workflow friction, and evaluate whether the solution provides a meaningful advantage.

## Key Product Learnings

Developing PreludeAI as a Product Management capstone provided an opportunity to explore how AI capabilities can be connected to a specific business workflow.

### Problem-Centric Product Design

A useful product begins with a clearly defined user problem rather than a technology looking for an application.

### Translating Problems Into Requirements

A broad challenge such as time-consuming sales research can be translated into specific capabilities, including information consolidation, summarization, and meeting preparation.

### Feature Prioritization

Not every potentially useful feature belongs in the first version of a product.

Prioritization helps focus development on capabilities most closely connected to the core user problem.

### Defining Success Before Measuring It

Product metrics should be linked to intended outcomes.

For PreludeAI, preparation time, brief usefulness, repeat usage, and factual accuracy provide possible ways to evaluate whether the solution delivers value.

### Responsible Use of Generative AI

AI-generated information should be evaluated for reliability, relevance, transparency, and usefulness.

These considerations are particularly important when the output may influence business conversations.
